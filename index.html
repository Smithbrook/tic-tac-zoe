Tic Tac Zoe

How to play

  • The board is a 3 x 3 grid. Player X always goes first.
  • Players take turns placing their mark in an empty square.
  • Get three of your marks in a row — across, down, or diagonally — to win.
  • If all nine squares fill up with no three-in-a-row, it's a draw.
  • In one-player mode you play X against the computer (O).
  • Pick a difficulty before starting: Easy, Medium, or Unbeatable.
